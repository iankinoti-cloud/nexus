Meet Amani

Amani runs a small design studio in Nairobi. Ten people work there. Businesses pay them to make logos, posters and videos.

Amani and the team of nine. Amani is made up for this talk. The problem is real.

Tool 2 of 7 · Enquiries

From phone call to paperwork in minutes

After every client call, Amani spends hours turning the conversation into a plan, a pitch and a price.

The fix: Amani pastes in what was said on the call. NEXUS writes all three in a few minutes. Amani reads them, fixes anything that’s off, and sends them.

From phone call to paperwork in minutes

Tool 3 of 7 · Projects

Every job, and how far along it is

Once clients say yes, the studio juggles a dozen jobs at once. It’s easy to miss that one has fallen behind.

The fix: every job sits on one list with a bar showing how much is done, like a class register for projects. A late job gets a red “Running late” label.
