## CHAPTER 1 — THE PROBLEM
*[Screen shows Chapter 1: dark background, six tool chips (NOTION, SHEETS, SLACK, EMAIL, DRIVE, XERO) scattered with broken connection lines. Timestamp ~0:00–1:10]*

---

Every boutique creative agency runs on a contradiction.

They sell clarity — sharp briefs, cohesive campaigns, tight production — but behind the scenes, their own operations look like this.

Six tools. No connection. A discovery call ends, and someone — usually the most experienced person in the room — opens a blank document and starts transferring information from their notes into a proposal. Then into a quote. Then into a project tracker. Then back into Slack to chase a brief that never made it out of someone's inbox.

That's two to four hours of senior creative time. Per client. And boutique agencies handle ten, fifteen new enquiries a month.

Do the math. That's an entire working week of your best people doing admin, every single month.

Nobody talks about this. But it's the reason agencies plateau. Not because they lack talent. Because the system they're running on was never designed for them.

## CHAPTER 2 — THE EVIDENCE
*[Screen shows Chapter 2: "ONE SYSTEM." with the six NEXUS modules listed — Projects, Talent, Clients, Enquiries, Core, Analytics. Timestamp ~1:10–2:20]*

---

NEXUS is the operating system built specifically for this kind of business.

One workspace. Every part of your agency — connected, intelligent, and in one place.

Projects tracks your active pipeline: budgets, timelines, risk flags — updated in real time. Talent monitors your team's utilization and burnout risk so you're never blindsided by a team member hitting the wall two days before a major delivery.

Clients gives you a full relationship dossier on every client you've ever worked with — health scoring, deal history, communication signals. Enquiries takes a new inbound brief all the way from RFP to proposal to invoice without ever leaving the platform.

And Analytics keeps the financial picture clear — revenue trends, margin per project, Q3 forecasts — not as a report you generate at the end of the month, but as a live view you can check between calls.

Everything feeds everything else. That's the difference.

## CHAPTER 3 — THE FORMULA
*[Screen shows Chapter 3: CORE AI — four agent cards (Strategy, Creative, Operations, Financial). Timestamp ~2:20–3:30]*

---

At the center of NEXUS is Core.

Core is not a chatbot. It's a team of four specialized AI agents, each focused on a specific domain of your business.

The Strategy agent detects scope creep, burnout risk, and revenue gaps — before they turn into problems you're managing under pressure.

The Creative agent drafts briefs, proposals, and scopes of work in seconds. The 3-agent pipeline — Brief Extractor, Proposal Generator, Quote Generator — runs in sequence, each step specialized, each step handing off structured context to the next. The result: a proposal that sounds like it was written by your senior creative, because it was trained on how your senior creative thinks.

The Operations agent rebalances workloads, re-slots deadlines, and keeps production moving when reality doesn't match the plan.

And the Financial agent tracks margin per project, forecasts pipeline revenue, and flags billing delays before they become cash flow problems.

Four specialists. One interface. Always on.

## CHAPTER 4 — THE PALETTE
*[Screen shows Chapter 4: four color-block stages expanding — ENQUIRY, PROPOSAL, PRODUCTION, INVOICE. Timestamp ~3:30–4:20]*

---

Here's what a client engagement looks like inside NEXUS.

A brief comes in. Core reads it, extracts the objectives and deliverables, and generates a scoped proposal with a line-item quote — aligned to the client's stated budget — in under three minutes.

If the client says yes, production spins up automatically. Talent is assigned. The timeline is locked. Equipment and studio bookings go into the schedule.

When the work is approved, the invoice goes out. Not because someone remembered to send it. Because the system knows the work is done.

Enquiry to invoice — one continuous flow. No context lost between steps. No handoff friction.
